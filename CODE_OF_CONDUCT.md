# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, technically rigorous, and welcoming to people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome correction and explain disagreements clearly;
- protect private recordings, transcripts, credentials, and personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and
unwelcome sexual or personal conduct are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may
remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the
project constructive.
